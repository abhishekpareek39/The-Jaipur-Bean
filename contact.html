Frequently Asked Queries

Q. Do you offer high-speed Wi-Fi for remote work?

Yes! We provide complimentary high-speed fiber Wi-Fi access along with dedicated power sockets beneath our workspace bench seating areas.

Q. Can we host private birthday parties or corporate meetings?

Absolutely. You can select the "Weekend Event / Party Hosting" option in the form above, and our management team will reach out with customized layout options and menu pricing packages.

Q. Is vehicle parking available near the C-Scheme cafe?

We have designated two-wheeler parking directly in front of the cafe gate, alongside peaceful roadside four-wheeler parking slots on Sardar Patel Marg.

Q. Do you have vegan or dairy-free alternative milk choices?

Yes, we highly prioritize dietary preferences. Our expert baristas can prepare any of our hot espresso drinks or cold brews with premium almond milk or oat milk upon request.
